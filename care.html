Setup & care.

Keep the housing. Replace the carbon. Follow the instructions included with your product if they differ from this preliminary online guide.

First-time setup

  1. Locate the oval-shaped opening on the ceramic carbon chamber and the feet on the lid shaft.
  2. Fill the chamber with the specified granular activated carbon.
  3. Place the lid on top and rotate clockwise until the feet meet their stops and the lid locks.
  4. Align the chamber lock with the spout of a compatible cone-top carafe; insert and rotate approximately a quarter turn to secure it.
  5. Run water through to remove loose carbon dust. The first water may appear cloudy or dark. Flush until clear, according to product instructions.
Important: Use an appropriate water source. Activated carbon filtration alone does not establish that unsafe water is microbiologically safe to drink.

Removing the filter

Rotate the chamber until its lock aligns with the spout, then lift it out. If the lid is tight, gently loosen it and rotate away from its stop.

Carbon replacement

The instructions Scott supplied specify monthly replacement. Other earlier promotional materials mention different timing. Follow the most recent approved instructions included with your product until a single verified replacement interval is confirmed.

Cleaning and pour-over coffee

Rinse or gently clean the ceramic housing as directed. For pour-over coffee, remove the water filtration insert, then use the appropriate paper filter and coffee grounds with the compatible carafe.

Carbon refill options

Chimera offers 2 oz and 12 oz replacement carbon packages. Current prices, product photographs, and separate purchase links remain to be confirmed.
