Inside the design

The idea is simple. The design is considered.

Instead of replacing an entire plastic cartridge, Chimera holds activated carbon in a reusable ceramic chamber. The chamber locks into a compatible glass carafe.

Meet the inventor

Six years. Thirty-two prototypes. A different way to filter.

Industrial designer and patent agent Scott Keeley developed Chimera around one question: Why throw away a plastic cartridge when the carbon inside is the part that needs replacing?

His ceramic-and-glass approach turns a disposable habit into a reusable everyday object.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Does the glass carafe come with it?

The system and insert may be sold separately. Check the exact listing and chosen configuration before placing an order.

Does Chimera work for pour-over coffee?

The carafe can support pour-over preparation after removing the ceramic water-filter insert and using appropriate coffee grounds and a paper filter.

How often should I replace the carbon?

Follow the instructions included with your purchased product. The provided materials contain differing historical replacement intervals, so the definitive schedule needs seller confirmation.

Can it filter unsafe water?

No. Do not use the product as a substitute for treatment of microbiologically unsafe water. Specific contaminant reduction claims need verified test evidence.

How do I set it up?

See our setup and care guide for the twist-lock instructions and first-use carbon rinse.
